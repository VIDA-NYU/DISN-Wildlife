Julia von Ferber, Ph.D. is an Assistant Professor for the Department of Security, Fire and Emergency Management (SFEM) at John Jay College of Criminal Justice, CUNY. Her research focuses on applying Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED) to measure crime in privately owned public spaces and cybercrime.
